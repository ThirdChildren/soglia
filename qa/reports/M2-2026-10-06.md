## Findings and warnings

- **F1 (needs an owner decision, not a blocker)**: S2.1 verification 8 ("with the pinch active no `menu opened`"): in IWER a `xr_set_select_value 1` followed within about 1 s by the palm-up pose still produced `menu opened hand=left` and a `menu closed` 0.58 s later (twice, with 0 s and 0.6 s head start); with 1.5 s of head start nothing opened. `PalmMenuSystem` reads the pinch from `selectstart`/`selectend` (`src/systems/pinch-input.ts`), so the app learns about the pinch late in IWER. The cause (IWER event latency vs a real delay in `isPinching`) is not established here. Hypothesis: IWER only dispatches `selectstart` once the pinch value settles; sequence for the rerun: pinch, wait 1.5 s, palm up. If the scenario text should hold literally, the order ("pinch first, then palm up within 0.2 s") must be updated or the app must be checked.
- **F2**: `scene_get_render_stats` throws `Cannot read properties of undefined (reading 'x')` while the palm menu is open (reproduced 3 times, also after the held piece was released; works with the menu closed). Tool/bridge issue, no console error from the app. Impact: the worst-case numbers can only come from the `[soglia:stats]` line.
- **F3**: IWER settling of the pinch (about 1.2 cm of grip drift, plan `Esiti` T2.13) still leaves residuals of 0.05-0.10 plan metres on moved pieces (S2.3 x=7.05 vs 7.00, S2.6 z=2.60 vs 2.50). The pieces end valid and in the right room; scenario tolerances of +-0.02 are not reachable with the current correction on moves of the model pieces. Suggest widening the tolerance of these positions to +-0.10 or computing them from the placed log.
- **F4**: S2.9 steps 10-11 starting points collide with pieces at scale 0.12 in the preset (as R15/trap 3 of the plan); the scenario text should say "hands 0.10 m above the floor of the model" for these two steps.
- **W1 (budget)**: worst case 74 calls/view > internal target 70 (limit 100). `docs/plans/M2.md` records 73 for the non-zoomed case; here 74 with zoom 0.12.
