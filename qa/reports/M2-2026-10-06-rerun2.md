- **Reproduce**: `house=apartment-a&role=visitor&reset=1&seed=1&debug=1`; accept the session, hand mode; open the menu with the left hand (`(-0.25; 1.15; -0.2)`, `Q_UP`), wait 1.5 s; move the left hand to `(0.0282; 1.5522; -0.5288)` (same `Q_UP`; the menu follows the palm and `ui:menu-page-next` ends at about (0.0681; 1.5347; -0.4948), the pinch point of the left hand is then inside its rectangle); `xr_select {device: "hand-left", duration: 0.2}`. - **Obtained** (first reproduction, timestamps): `pinch left start` ...666666, `menu page 2/3 items=coffee-table,desk,bookcase,wardrobe,nightstand,tv-stand` ...671, `menu closed` ...672, then **six** `error` lines within 2 ms: `[PanelUISystem] Failed to load panel for entity 50/51/52/53/54/55: TypeError: Cannot read properties of undefined (reading 'add')  at PanelUISystem.loadPanel (.../node_modules/@iwsdk/core/dist/ui/ui.js:90:25)`. - **Not reproduced**: five page changes with the **other** (right) hand over the whole run (0 errors); the same left-hand pinch while a piece was held (no page change: the menu is dimmed, `furniture rotated ... rot=90` instead) gave no error; the `Undo` pinch with the left hand while a piece was held gave no error.

Entities involved: the six `ui:menu-item-*` of page 2 and `ui:palm-menu`.

## What the emulator cannot prove (to add to `qa/device/DEBT.md`)

- Real hand precision: pinch point (IWER's `gripSpace` sits about 5 cm from the set pose and drifts 1.2 cm while the pinch closes), jitter, the new **rectangle pick** of the menu controls (6.1 x 4.7 cm items, 4.4 x 3.5 cm buttons half sizes) with fingers and its false-positive rate between neighbouring buttons (0.092 m apart), the 0.30 m snap radius at 1:20, the 0.045 m / 0.05 m pick radii.
- Palm-up detection with real hands on both hands (`PALM_NORMAL_LOCAL` is IWER-specific `+X`), accidental openings, the 0.4 s palm time and the 300 ms guard (first guesses), the pinch-before-palm order with real fingers, the "palm too short" case (8e).
- The menu follows the palm: how much it moves with real hand tremor and whether it stays inside the 30 deg cone; the **0.50 m minimum distance** with the narrow field of view of the Meta VR Glasses; legibility of icons with text at 0.50-0.59 m; the **dimmed menu (35 % opacity)** legibility and the label drawn above it; whether a user understands a dimmed, non-selectable menu while holding a piece.
- Wrist rotation reliability (50/40 deg hysteresis) versus the tap of the other hand; comfort of rotating seated.
- Real fps and real draw calls/triangles on the Quest with 14 pieces, the menu open and a piece held (**85 calls/view on the PC**), the block fallback (63), the cost of UIKit panels.
- One-handed use of the menu (palm up and pinch with the same hand): the same-hand pinch closes the menu in the same frame as the control action, and in the emulator it triggers F1 on a page change.
- Real microphone/passthrough/network behaviour: none exercised.
