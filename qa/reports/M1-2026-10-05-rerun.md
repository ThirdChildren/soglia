## What the emulator CANNOT prove (to add to `qa/device/DEBT.md`; DEBT.md was NOT modified from here)

2. Precision and reliability of real hand pinch (two-hand zoom/rotation, room pinch, deadzone/thresholds, jitter) and the comfort of the zoom limits.
3. Real fps (>= 60 on the Quest), real draw calls and triangles via remote debugging (the PC shows 21.3-21.7k triangles for two views, above the internal target of 20 000), time per frame, GPU cost of the UIKit panels.
4. Readability of the room label and error panel text at 0.5-0.8 m, presence of the glyphs `·` and `²` in the font on screen, narrow field of view of the Meta VR Glasses.
5. Onboarding: the ghost hand is understood without explanation by a person who does not know the app; its size, distance, animation speed; the exact 1.0 s of quiet before it appears; the 9.0 s timeout feels right for a one-handed user.
6. Real seated height and distance of the miniature (the emulator places O.y = head - 0.25 at session start with a head at 1.6 m).
7. Dimensions of the UIKit font atlases (256x512 per the scenario text) and the exact texture memory: only a device/GPU profile can measure them.
