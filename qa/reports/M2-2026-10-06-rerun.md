## Warnings

- **W1 (budget)**: worst case 72 calls/view > internal target 70 (limit 100): "AVVISO: 72 > 70, accepted" by the user; go to the device debt as "measure on the Quest". Run 4 (blocks) uses **63 calls/view versus 49 with the models**, i.e. the fallback path costs 14 more draw calls and draws about 4,700 fewer triangles; still far from 100, but the "blocks are cheaper" expectation in the scenario does not hold (probably more meshes per block, e.g. an outline or a base mesh; not traced).
- **W2 (central view, rule 8; new)**: the 30 degree cone fix moved the palm menu to the middle of the view, 0.56 m from the head, **in front of the far half of the model** in the default view (head at 1.6 m looking forward). With the palm up the menu hides the bedroom/study side of the plan: the bed is mostly invisible in the S2.2 and S2.5 screenshots, and the reason labels (always placed 0.46-0.59 m from the head, near the piece) end up partly behind the menu buttons: "Overlaps th... robe" (S2.3), only "...or" of "Blocks the door" (S2.6), "...ouble bed" (S2.8). The scenario criteria are met by data (positions, logs), but a user with the palm up cannot read the reason text unless he looks from another angle. Not measured on the headset: the real menu only stays open while the palm is up, and the model could be viewed from a lower angle. Needs an owner decision (e.g. order of the panels, offsetting the menu away from the model, or hiding the menu while a piece is held) and a device check.
- **W3 (UI)**: the label of the "Three-seat sofa" button touches the border of its button in the 800x800 view (the "t" and "a" almost cut). Hand-readability to be checked on the device.
- **W4 (visual)**: panels rotated toward the head show their text slanted (italic look): "Living room & kitchen", "Double bed", "Armchair", "Overlaps the armchair"; no log explains it, same observation as W2 of the previous report; check on the headset.

- **W6 (bundle)**: `npm run build` warns the single chunk is 6,672 kB (1,713.81 kB gzip) versus 6,667 kB in the previous gate (+5 kB); not a gate criterion.
